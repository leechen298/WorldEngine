# Intent

Status: final / closeout complete

## Why This Exists

Final closeout is the point where the v0.5 campaign either has enough current
evidence to close or must remain open. It must not rely on historical v0.4
evidence and must not overstate unrun validation surfaces.

## Outcomes

- final closeout record.
- final verification evidence.
- final unresolved finding classification.
- parent and roadmap status synchronization after evaluator approval.
- explicit next-version handoff boundary.

## Non-Goals

- no implementation changes.
- no release tag or push.
- no v0.6, v0.7, or v0.8 work.
- no frontend, E2E, Agent smoke, autonomous, or external validation readiness
  claim unless directly verified here.

## Handoff

After final closeout, v0.6 world generation v1 may start only from its own
reviewed iteration package. v0.5 does not implement v0.6 scope.
